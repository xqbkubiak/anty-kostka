## 📌 About

**Anty-Kostka** is an intelligent inventory management tool designed for players who value organization and speed. It allows you to "lock in" your preferred inventory layout and restore it instantly with a single shortcut.

Whether you're preparing for a boss fight, organizing your building materials, or just keeping your hotbar clean, Anty-Kostka ensures your items are always exactly where they belong.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💾 Inventory Management
- **Full Layout Save** - Save your entire inventory configuration (Hotbar, Main Inventory, and Off-hand) with one click.
- **Single Slot Save** - Hover over a specific slot in any GUI to save only that individual item position.
- **Smart Restoration** - Automatically moves items from your inventory to their saved positions using optimized click sequences.

### ⚙️ Functionality
- **Cross-Version Support** - Seamlessly works across Minecraft versions from 1.21.1 up to 1.21.10.
- **Intuitive Controls** - Minimalist design that stays out of your way until you need it.
- **Visual Feedback** - Real-time chat notifications confirming your saves and restoration status.

---

## 🎮 Usage

### ⚡ Keybinds (Default)

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl + I` | **Save Layout** (Full inventory or hovered slot) |
| `Ctrl + O` | **Restore Layout** (Toggle automatic restoration) |

### 🧩 Commands

| Command | Action |
|---------|--------|
| `/bks help` | Show help menu and available shortcuts |
| `/bks save` | Quick hint on how to save your layout |

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest `.jar` for your Minecraft version.
2. Ensure you have [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) installed.
3. Place the mod into your `%appdata%/.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and use `Ctrl + I` to save your first layout!
